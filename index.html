Social Finance

SPLIT BILLS &
TRACK BALANCES

Split any shared expense fairly with six split methods. Track who owes what across all peers. Settle up instantly with real-time notifications and balance tracking.

SPLIT

  • Bills tab lists individual splits with status
  • Settlement tracking per participant
  • Nudge button for 24-hour reminders
  • Comments on any bill
  • Full conversation history per split
